# Outmake

Run local AI models on your Mac. Chat, pictures, video, voices and dictation, free and with no account.

Outmake downloads open models from Hugging Face and Civitai and runs them on your Mac. Before you download one, it checks your Mac's memory and marks it Runs great, Runs well or Too big. Chat runs on llama.cpp, pictures and video on stable-diffusion.cpp, and voices on MLX.

**[Download at outmake.app](https://outmake.app)** · Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later

```sh
brew install --cask outmakeapp/tap/outmake
```
